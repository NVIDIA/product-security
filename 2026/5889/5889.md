### Security Bulletin - NVIDIA AIStore Framework - October 2026

Updated 2026-10-13

NVIDIA has released a software update for NVIDIA® AIStore™ framework.&nbsp;<div>&nbsp;To protect your system, download and install the 5.0 version of the NVIDIA AIStore framework from&nbsp;<a href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/aistore">NVIDIA/aistore</a> Github.</div>

A successful exploit of this vulnerability might lead to information disclosure. | [CVSS:3.1<br>/AV:N<br>/AC:L<br>/PR:N<br>/UI:N<br>/S:C<br>/C:H<br>/I:N<br>/A:N](https://www.first.org/cvss/calculator/3.1#CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:C/C:H/I:N/A:N) | 8.6 | HIGH | [CWE-918](https://cwe.mitre.org/data/definitions/918.html) | Information disclosure |

A successful exploit of this vulnerability might lead to code execution, escalation of privileges, information disclosure, data tampering, and denial of service. | [CVSS:3.1<br>/AV:N<br>/AC:H<br>/PR:N<br>/UI:N<br>/S:U<br>/C:H<br>/I:H<br>/A:H](https://www.first.org/cvss/calculator/3.1#CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:H/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H) | 8.1 | HIGH | [CWE-22](https://cwe.mitre.org/data/definitions/22.html) | Code execution, escalation of privileges, data tampering, denial of service, information disclosure |
